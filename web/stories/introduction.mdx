# Flashtap

Learning games that progress by ability, built on [strata-react](https://github.com/jburnhams/strata-react). Each
story is a whole screen, opened through the app's own route tree at a URL, with the server put into
one state by MSW (the same handlers the jsdom tests and the visual suite use). Use the toolbar to
switch between light and dark.

- **Play**: a round per kind (`?mode=`), its prompt as the `PageHeader` title, large tiles from
  strata-react's `ChoiceGrid`, a `NativeSelect` for the kind of round with its choice in the URL.
- **Content**: the content packs for the grown-up. A `MetricStrip`, `Tabs` (Skills, Curriculum,
  Phonics) with the tab in the URL, a `DataTable` of skills whose rows open in place (`useExpanded`,
  open rows in the URL) to show their links, and `Status` for a curriculum goal that is out of scope.
- **Settings**: `Tabs` with the tab in the URL, a form started from the server's values
  (`NativeSelect`, `NumberField`, `AsyncButton`), `ColorSchemeChoice`.

Every story is screenshotted in Chromium, light and dark, and compared with a committed baseline in
CI (`npm run test:visual`). States reached by a tap (a wrong tile, the right one) are route shots
in `strata-visual.config.ts`.
